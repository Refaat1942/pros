1. Purpose of This Document

This blueprint explains what the system does, who uses it, and how a patient case moves from reception to delivery. It is written for center management, department heads, auditors, and IT staff who need a clear picture without reading source code.

2. System Purpose & Scope

Smart Prosthetics ERP is an integrated enterprise system for a prosthetics manufacturing center. It manages:

  • Clinical records — patient registration, appointments, medical exams, measurements
  • Operational cases — one case per prosthetic order, tracked through all departments
  • Inventory — stock receiving, barcode dispense, bill of materials (BOM)
  • Finance — quotes, contract approvals, payments, contract debts, military sovereign debt
  • Governance — immutable audit trail and five BI command boards
Offline-first design: The system runs 100% on a local server inside the center. Daily work does not require internet. Patient data never leaves the local network. Staff PCs need only a web browser (Chrome recommended).

3. Core Design Principles

PrincipleMeaning for the center
Path separationCivilian and military patients follow different financial rules. Data and reports stay isolated by patient type.
Event-driven workflowCases move automatically when a department completes its work. No manual “send to next department” buttons.

Barcode for stockEvery dispense must match the approved BOM. Wrong scans are blocked and logged.

4. Registration & Classification

At reception, every patient receives:

  1. A permanent Patient ID stored in the database
  2. A printed patient card with QR code for queue and status tracking
  3. A classification: Civilian or Military

For civilians, the receptionist also links the patient to a contract company (insurer, union, employer) with coverage rules. For military patients, rank and sovereign entity are recorded instead.

5. Shared Clinical Path (Both Types)

Reception → Medical exam → Technical specification → Adjustments → Cost calculation ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ Patient file Doctor diagnosis Spec engineer picks Fitting items Pricing engine + QR card + prescription components (codes confirmed runs in background only — no prices)

Blind specification rule

During technical specification, the specialist sees item codes and quantities only. Purchase prices and warehouse balances are hidden so clinical decisions stay independent of finance and stock pressure.

6. Civilian Path — Full Financial Gate

StepWhat happens
1. Quote issuedPDF quote with amount in words + QR code. Case is financially frozen until approval.
2. Contract approvalPatient obtains official approval letter from contract entity. Reception scans/uploads it (OCR-assisted validation).
3. Operations releaseOn successful validation, case unfreezes and receives a central work order number.
4. Cash patients (optional)Patients without a contract company pass through the cashier for payment before manufacturing.
5. Warehouse dispenseStorekeeper scans barcodes against the approved BOM. Mismatch = blocked + audit alert.
6. ManufacturingWorkshop completes production stages (generation → assembly → casting → finishing).
7. DeliveryReception scans patient QR at delivery. Case closes. Invoice/debt posts to contract entity.

7. Military Path — Financial Bypass

StepWhat happens
After cost calculationNo quote and no payment wait. Cost is calculated silently for reporting only.
Services approval (optional)Certain beneficiary categories may require an internal services approval step before operations.
OperationsCase auto-advances to operations and is auto-approved for manufacturing.
Manufacturing & deliverySame warehouse barcode rules and workshop stages as civilian path.
Financial closeCost rolls to sovereign military debt reports — not patient billing.
Important: Civilian and military data must never be mixed in reports, queries, or UI filters without explicit path scoping. This is a non-negotiable security and compliance requirement.

11. Full Business Cycle (Connected)

① RECEPTION Register patient → create Case → print QR card ↓ ② CLINICAL Exam → Spec (codes/qty) → Adjustments ↓ ③ PRICING Background engine calculates TWO totals per line: • Quote price = qty × Highest Purchase Price (sales basis) • Internal cost = qty × WAC (true inventory cost) ↓ ④ COSTING Add overhead components + profit margin → selling price → Quote (civilian) ↓ ⑤ APPROVAL Contract entity approves quote (civilian) OR auto-release (military) ↓ ⑥ OPERATIONS Work order issued → BOM created (raw) → qty reserved on stock ↓ ⑦ WAREHOUSE Barcode scan each item → StockMovement (issue) → qty DEDUCTED from stock → WIP stage → Civilian: contract debt POSTED on dispense ↓ ⑧ WORKSHOP Manufacturing sub-stages → BOM finished ↓ ⑨ DELIVERY Patient QR scan → case closed → Military: sovereign debt posted → Civilian cash: payment recorded at cashier (if applicable) ↓ ⑩ FINANCE Debt collection reduces contract balance · Profit report compares revenue (quote/selling price) vs internal cost (WAC)

13. Multiple Prices Per Item — Why Three Numbers Exist

Price typeSourceUsed for
Purchase batchesEach inward receipt creates a StockItemPrice row (supplier, invoice, unit price, qty)Price history; highest-price lookup
Highest Purchase PriceMax of all batches with qty > 0 (and base catalog price)Civilian quote / selling price — protects center from repurchase inflation
WAC (Moving Average)(prior_qty × prior_WAC + in_qty × in_price) ÷ (prior_qty + in_qty)Inventory valuation, internal cost, profit margin calculation
Selling priceMaterials (highest price) + overhead components + profit %Patient/entity invoice; revenue in profitability report
Finance clarity: WAC is never mixed into civilian quotes. Quotes use highest purchase price. Profit is measured as revenue (selling price) minus issue cost (WAC at barcode dispense). Admin reports include Inventory ↔ Finance Reconciliation and Item Profit Margins (WAC vs highest price per SKU).

20. Costing Engine → Quote (Civilian Sales Price)

materials_total = Σ (qty × highest purchase price) ← from pricing engine + overhead = configurable % components (labour, etc.) = total_cost × (1 + profit_rate%) = selling_price → becomes Quote.total → contract debt basis

Military path: same internal WAC cost stored on case; no quote document; sovereign debt uses WAC-based total_cost.

21. Finance Department Controls

  • Every stock movement is immutable (StockMovement) with WAC unit cost and balance_after snapshot
  • At dispense, all BOM lines refresh to current WAC — same basis for stock deduction and issue_cost
  • Every financial posting writes to audit log (tag: financial)
  • Duplicate posting prevented (ledger_posted_at atomic claim on case)
  • Credit notes for post-delivery civilian adjustments (admin approved)
  • Civilian and military figures never combined in one report row without explicit label

24. Immutable Audit Log

Every create, update, workflow transition, blocked barcode scan, and financial posting is logged automatically with:

  • User identity and timestamp (to the second)
  • Device identifiers (IP / MAC where available)
  • Action type and module tag
  • Full before/after data snapshot
Legal-grade trail: Audit records are append-only. No user — including system administrator or IT — can modify or delete entries through the application.

25. Master Data (Configured Once)

  • Contract companies and coverage rules
  • Military ranks and beneficiary categories
  • Stock catalog, categories, suppliers, kits
  • Visit types, pathway steps, costing modes
  • Users, roles, and permission defaults

26. Document Summary

Smart Prosthetics ERP connects clinical care, manufacturing, inventory, and finance in one offline system. Sales (quotes) deduct from stock only through validated barcode dispense. Multiple purchase prices per item feed both quote pricing (highest) and inventory valuation (WAC moving average). Finance receives opening/closing balances, profitability margins, and purchasing erosion alerts. Civilian and military paths remain fully separated in data and reports.
